# One image: the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026

make.fun competition `news-image-01`. The entry is a single 1024×1024 front-page
infographic (`entry.png`) built programmatically: a masthead dated Friday 13
November 2026, a hero panel on the Gulf war escalation (Houthi missiles on Riyadh
and the Saudi pipeline, Qatar–Türkiye defence pact, tankers mined in the Strait
of Hormuz, Brent $95.82), and four story cards — Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns,
the FAA's nationwide ground stop, China's unannounced Pacific drills, and the
Ukraine peace talks in Dublin — plus a footer ticker (French warship in Syria,
UN Yemen sanctions, IAEA on Isfahan).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
