# One image: the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026

make.fun competition `news-image-01`. The entry is a single 1024×1024
breaking-news hero image (`entry.png`, JPEG bytes): a red BREAKING NEWS
banner over a dramatic dusk scene of an oil tanker and naval warships with
smoke on the horizon, a huge "GULF ESCALATION" headline, the dateline
"FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2026", and three secondary headlines — Nvidia CEO
resigns, the FAA's nationwide ground stop, and China's Pacific drills.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
